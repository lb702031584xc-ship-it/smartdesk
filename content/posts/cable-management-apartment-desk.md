Visible cables make a compact setup feel unfinished. The fix is usually mechanical, not aesthetic.

## The one-tray method

Install an under-desk tray, mount a power strip inside it, and drop every brick into that zone. From the floor, you should mostly see one clean power cord to the wall.

## Label before you bundle

Mark laptop, monitor, and lamp cables before sleeves go on. Future you will thank present you when something needs replacing.

## Vertical overflow

If the desk surface is still busy, move notebooks and headphones to a wall shelf in the primary reach zone—about eye level when seated.
