## Setup notes

Place the desk so the rear edge has clamp clearance for a monitor arm. In narrow rooms, orient the long side parallel to the wall and keep at least 30 inches behind the chair for a clean push-back path.

## Day-to-day use

Memory presets matter more than you expect. Switching postures mid-afternoon is easier when you are not hunting for the right height each time.

## Who should skip it

If you want a furniture-like solid wood piece that never moves, a shallow writing desk may suit the room better—and cost less.
