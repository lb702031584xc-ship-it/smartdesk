A forty-inch desk can feel cramped fast—unless every inch has a job.

## Start with the work triangle

Keep your keyboard, primary notebook, and drink in a tight triangle so you are not reaching across the full depth of the desk all day. Push secondary items (chargers, spare pens, sticky notes) to a wall shelf or under-desk tray.

## Raise the screen, free the surface

A monitor arm or slim riser is usually the highest-leverage upgrade. Eye-level screens reduce neck strain and reclaim the zone under the display for a laptop dock or notepad.

## Choose depth carefully

Many "compact" desks are still 24 inches deep. If your chair needs clear kneespace and your room is narrow, prioritize 20–22 inches of depth and a chair that tucks fully under the desktop.

## Cable discipline in one evening

1. Mount a power strip under the desk.
2. Route monitor and laptop cables into a single sleeve.
3. Leave one accessible USB-C cable for guests or phone charging.

## What to buy first

If you can only change one thing, change seating or screen height before buying decorative organizers. Comfort compounds; clutter trays do not.
