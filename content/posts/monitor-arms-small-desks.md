A small desk becomes usable again the moment the monitor leaves the surface.

## Clamp vs grommet

Clamp mounts are ideal for rented apartments because they avoid drilling. Confirm your desktop thickness and leave enough rear overhang for the clamp. Grommounts look cleaner but need a hole.

## One screen vs two

For writing and browser work, a single 27-inch panel on an arm often beats dual 24s in a tight footprint. If you need dual screens, mount both on one base when possible to reduce clamp clutter.

## Do not ignore cable service loops

Leave enough slack for full height and tilt travel. Bind excess under the desk so the arm can move without tugging the power brick.

## Budget path

If an arm is not in budget yet, a solid riser plus an external keyboard still improves neck angle and frees laptop space underneath. Upgrade to an arm when you are ready to reclaim the full depth.
