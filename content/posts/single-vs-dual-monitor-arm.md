Monitor arms are less about brand theater and more about **screen count** and **desk depth**. This comparison uses two products already in the SmartDesk catalog: a dual arm (legacy product JSON) and a compact single arm (Product Schema V1). Scores, prices, and affiliate destinations stay on those product records.

## Quick take

- **Dual arm** wins when two monitors currently sit on the desktop and you need both raised.
- **Single arm** wins when one external display is enough and you want a smaller mount footprint.

## Decision checklist

1. How many monitors need to leave the desk?
2. Does the rear edge have clamp clearance?
3. Is the top thick enough for a clamp (or do you need a grommet mount)?

## Common mistakes

- Buying a dual arm for a one-monitor desk “just in case”
- Ignoring cable slack after raising both screens
- Pushing the desk flush to the wall so the clamp cannot fit
