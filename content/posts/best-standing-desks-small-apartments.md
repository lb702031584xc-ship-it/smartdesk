## Who this guide is for

This ranking is for people setting up a real workday in a small room—not a showroom office. If you need the desk to disappear visually, or share space with living furniture, start here.

## What we valued most

1. **Footprint** that leaves a walkway
2. **Stability** at standing height
3. **Cable paths** that keep the floor clear
4. **Assembly** you can finish without specialty tools

## Buying tip

Buy the desk first, then the chair. A great chair cannot fix a desktop that forces you into a cramped posture against a wall.
